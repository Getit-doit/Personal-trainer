## Part 2 — Body composition across the body-fat spectrum (16 entries)

- **body_fat_exercise_selection_myth** — Ramírez-Campillo R et al. spot-reduction systematic review/meta-analysis (no localized fat reduction); ACSM Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription (modality/intensity, not BF%, drive prescription); WHO 2020.
- **body_fat_reference_ranges** — American Council on Exercise (ACE) body-fat categories; ACSM body-composition norms; Gallagher D et al., Am J Clin Nutr 2000.
- **measuring_body_fat_methods** — Aragon AA et al., ISSN: Diets and Body Composition, 2017; DEXA-vs-BIA validation literature; skinfold/Navy-method reliability studies.
- **training_high_body_fat_beginner** — ACSM exercise-prescription guidance for adults with obesity; Donnelly JE et al., ACSM Position Stand on physical activity for weight loss, Med Sci Sports Exerc 2009; 5–10% weight-loss risk-reduction benchmark.
- **cardio_higher_body_fat** — Bartels EM et al., Cochrane Review, Aquatic exercise for knee/hip osteoarthritis, 2016; Aquatic Exercise Association immersion/weight-bearing data.
- **resistance_training_higher_body_fat** — Weinheimer EM et al., Nutrition Reviews 2010 (~24% lean loss diet-only vs ~11% diet+exercise); 2025 BJSM systematic review on resistance exercise during weight loss; 25-RCT meta-analysis.
- **realistic_fat_loss_rate** — Garthe I et al., Int J Sport Nutr Exerc Metab 2011; ISSN (Aragon et al. 2017) on deficit size vs leanness.
- **protein_calorie_targets_leanness** — Jäger R et al., ISSN: Protein and Exercise, 2017; Helms ER et al. 2014 (2.3–3.1 g/kg FFM for lean dieters).
- **lean_getting_leaner** — Byrne NM et al., MATADOR, Int J Obes 2018; Campbell BI et al., carbohydrate refeed, J Funct Morphol Kinesiol 2020; Poon ET-C et al., diet-break meta-analysis, Nutrition Reviews 2024; IOC RED-S consensus.
- **very_low_body_fat_risks** — Mountjoy M et al., IOC Consensus on RED-S, Br J Sports Med 2014/2018 (impairment <~30 kcal/kg FFM/day); ACSM Female Athlete Triad position stand; De Souza MJ et al. triad consensus.
- **body_recomposition** — Barakat C et al., Body Recomposition, Strength Cond J 2020; Murphy C & Koehler K, energy deficit and lean-mass gains meta-analysis, 2021.
- **cardio_vs_lifting_higher_body_fat** — Resistance-training fat-loss meta-analyses (BJSM 2025; 25-RCT); WHO 2020 combined aerobic + strengthening guidance.
- **body_fat_distribution_visceral** — Android (visceral) vs gynoid (subcutaneous) adiposity and cardiometabolic-risk literature; waist-to-height-ratio studies (~0.5 cutoff).
- **exercise_intensity_scaling** — Wewege M et al., HIIT vs MICT in overweight/obese adults, Obesity Reviews 2017 (similar body comp, HIIT ~40% less time); Sanca-Valeriano et al. 2023; ACSM RPE/intensity guidance. HIIT safety data in obesity limited.
- **body_fat_vs_bmi_vs_waist** — BMI-limitation studies in athletes/muscular individuals (Nutrients 2025; NFL-player analyses); normal-weight-obesity ("skinny-fat") literature; ACSM.
- **mindset_adherence_body_fat** — NEDA descriptions of muscle dysmorphia/warning signs; muscle-dysmorphia and exercise-dependence literature (Trott et al. 2020).
